# General Troubleshooting Procedure
Document status: SYNTHETIC (authored for this portfolio project). Operating limits mirror the rules used to generate the AI4I 2020 dataset.
Failure type: General

## Step 1 - Define the Incident
Record the machine, time, symptom and operator report before touching any data.

## Step 2 - Collect Evidence
Gather recent sensor readings, maintenance history, past incidents on the same machine, and any model predictions. Note when each piece of evidence was recorded.

## Step 3 - Form Hypotheses
List every plausible mechanism. Consider tool wear, heat dissipation, power, overstrain and unexplained causes before choosing.

## Step 4 - Test Each Hypothesis
For each hypothesis, find evidence that supports it and evidence that contradicts it. Compare readings with the documented limit for that mechanism.

## Step 5 - Conclude Carefully
Rank hypotheses by evidence. If evidence is insufficient or two hypotheses are nearly tied, say so and recommend specific further checks instead of claiming certainty.

## Step 6 - Document
Record the evidence, the conclusion, its confidence and the follow-up actions.
